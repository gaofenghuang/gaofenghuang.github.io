Postdoctoral fellow · Harvard University

Gaofeng Huang

I am a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Mathematics at Harvard University. My research is in complex analysis and geometry.

Email Harvard profile Publications & talks (PDF)

Research

I study holomorphic automorphism groups of Stein manifolds, density properties, and holomorphic approximation. My work also concerns symplectic geometry, Calogero–Moser spaces, and the factorization of holomorphic matrices.
